Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Both final assets are saved in this workspace and used by the `/products` route.

- `public/images/products/table-bowls.png` — 1672 × 941 photographic hero.
- `public/images/products/ingredient-sprites.png` — 2172 × 724 transparent ingredient sheet, rendered as three separate sprite cells without modifying the source raster.
- `public/images/products/table-empty-six.png` — current 1672 × 941 empty six-bowl table base. All food is drawn as movable pieces rather than baked into this photograph.
- `public/images/products/catalogue-sprites.png` — current transparent sheet containing cashew, dehydrated orange and millet cutouts. The fruit imagery follows the dehydrated fruit category shown in the homepage catalogue. The original three-bowl table remains preserved as an unused earlier asset.

The six-bowl hero starts with 395 photographic food pieces inside the bowls. The same pieces fly out; no second set is created and no full food photograph remains underneath. The final scroll frame holds all six bowls empty. Cashews, dehydrated fruits and millets come from the existing homepage catalogue; paneer and ginger retain the user's requested original selection.
